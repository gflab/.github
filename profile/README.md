# FengGao Lab @ SYSU

We develop explainable, multimodal, and workflow-aware artificial intelligence
for cancer research and clinical care. Colorectal cancer is our flagship
clinical setting, connecting disease biology, medical imaging, treatment
response, longitudinal follow-up, and medical reasoning.

The lab is led by Professor Feng Gao at the Sixth Affiliated Hospital of Sun
Yat-sen University in Guangzhou, China.

## Research Focus

- Explainable omics and disease mechanisms
- Multimodal foundation models across radiology, pathology, and molecular data
- Clinical prediction and longitudinal imaging
- Data-efficient medical vision and computational surgery
- Human-auditable AI systems for medical and research workflows

## Repository Policy

This organization is the shared home for lab-maintained research software,
reproducible artifacts, collaboration repositories, and reusable templates.
Each repository should identify its maintainers, lifecycle status, license,
and reproducibility boundary.

Patient-level or otherwise sensitive research data do not belong in GitHub,
including private repositories. Clinical AI work remains subject to expert
review, institutional governance, and explicit human responsibility.
